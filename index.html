Key notes
  • CP4 (stock fuel): max recommended power level is 135hp.
  • 175hp tunes: DCR/CPX required (aftermarket pump — not CP4).
  • SOFT / SOTF: switch on the fly (change power/fueling instantly).
  • LBF: Low Boost Fueling (more responsive). Note: EGTs can net higher temps — not recommended for heavy towing.
  • Smokeyy: more smoke / coal. Hiss: “jet” sound (turbo veins open). Rumble: deeper idle (turbo veins closed).
  • Beta (EGT working): EGT monitoring via EGR Inlet PID. Factory EGT backoff disabled on 20–22; coolant/oil/trans backoffs still exist (oil ~254°F, trans ~260°F).
